Autopilot is enabled per market. When on, price guidance within a row's band is accepted automatically; everything else is queued for manual review in the Price Cockpit. Week rows inherit their market's rules — edit a value to override it (shown in pink), and use ↺ to go back to inheriting. Switching a market off routes every guidance to manual review.
